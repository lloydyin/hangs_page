I am a PhD student in Statistics at University College Dublin, supervised by Dr. Wagner Barreto-Souza and Prof. Paula Carroll. My doctoral research is conducted as part of Rínn AI.

My PhD research focuses on developing statistical models for directional and circular time series, with particular applications to wind direction analysis and forecasting. The aim is to develop methodologies that can better capture the distinctive structure and temporal dependence of directional data, and ultimately improve their modelling and forecasting.

More broadly, my research interests include time series analysis, stochastic processes, Monte Carlo methods, statistical modelling, and computational statistics.

Feel free to contact me if you have any questions or would like to collaborate!
